## Bring AI into your life

We are building KBP AI to help people use AI in the real world: understand incoming information, make decisions and carry tasks through to verified results.

The assistant is designed to keep relevant people, messages, documents and next actions connected over time. You choose its sources, permissions and spending limits.

## One assistant, scenarios you shape

Our direction includes:

- **Communication and follow-up:** receive selected messages, prepare replies and keep track of agreements.
- **Decisions with context:** bring relevant information together and examine a decision with your chosen AI models.
- **Action through connected tools:** work with calendars, maps, websites and other services through supported APIs, MCP connections and browser interactions.
- **Voice and your surroundings:** develop phone, Apple-device and smart-home scenarios, each with its own permissions and acceptance checks.

These are product goals. See [current status](https://github.com/WestCoastKBP/.github/blob/main/docs/STATUS.md) for what is publicly available.

## The factory is the first workspace

The factory is the first contour in our design: a workspace for developing and testing the assistant's capabilities. It is intended to help users add integrations and shape agents for specific tasks as the product grows.

Personal and Work spaces are designed to keep their data and permissions separate. The user should be able to see what is running, what needs a decision and what result was confirmed.

## Your devices, AI and budget

We are targeting iPhone and iPad as the main interface, with Linux and macOS deployment in mind. The runtime is intended to run on a computer you control or a hosted virtual machine.

Supported subscription connections and separately billed APIs have different requirements. We are designing configurable budgets for models and paid services. Local hosting does not make cloud-model processing local.

## Where we are today

**KBP AI is a prototype in development.** This repository presents the product direction. A public installer, implementation source release and reproducible product demo are not available here yet.

We will publish demonstrations with their version, account requirements, costs where measured and known limitations. We do not claim universal access to vendor products or working support for every scenario listed above.
