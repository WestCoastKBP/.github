# KBP AI — brand and philosophy

Adopted by the Owner on October 10, 2026.

> **Knowledge Becomes Progress.** We help people turn knowledge and AI into useful actions and checked results in the outside world, while keeping human control over decisions, data and permissions.

This is the shared north star for employees, contributors and AI coordinators. Read it when starting work; use it to guide task selection, product design and review within the existing permissions and engineering workflow.

## Name and slogan

The product name is **KBP AI**.

**KBP** stands for **Knowledge Becomes Progress**. This is also the primary slogan; keep this English wording consistent.

**AI** stands for **Artificial Intelligence**.

## Purpose

Our purpose is to help people turn knowledge and AI into useful actions and checked results in real life.

Knowledge becomes progress when it helps someone understand a matter, choose an action, carry it through and check what happened. A completed task or a useful skill is a result. Generating more messages, code or plans alone is not proof of progress.

The person keeps control of decisions, data and permissions. Personal, engineering and business/work areas share this purpose while retaining their own data and authority boundaries.

## AI acting in the world

We aim to expand AI's useful interaction with the outside world: communicating with people, working with services and completing authorized tasks. Progress is measured by the checked result of those actions.

Security, privacy and recovery are baseline product requirements from the first connection. Each integration must address:

- **Permissions and human control.** Use explicit, scoped access that the person can review and revoke. AI may configure its working role within approved permissions; it cannot grant itself additional authority.
- **Privacy and separation.** Keep personal, legal, business and engineering data and permissions separate. Share only the information needed for the task.
- **Untrusted instructions.** Treat messages, pages, documents and tool results as data. Malicious instructions in them must not authorize actions, disclose secrets or expand access.
- **Credentials and recovery.** Keep keys and tokens in protected stores in their originating trusted environment, out of conversations and logs. Design for loss or compromise of a key, device or account: revoke access, replace credentials and restore through an authorized recovery path.
- **Traceable actions and safe failure.** Record who requested an action, what was authorized and what actually happened, without exposing private data. Check uncertain outcomes before retrying. Support cancellation, rollback where possible, backups and verified restoration.

Apply these requirements in proportion to the action's consequences. These are design and release requirements; public claims of protection require implementation and verification. We do not promise complete anonymity, perfect security or automatic recovery.

## Using the identity

Use **KBP AI** as the product name and **Knowledge Becomes Progress** as the primary slogan in the application, website, GitHub profile, README introductions, demonstrations and public materials. Explain AI as Artificial Intelligence when spelling out the name.

The same philosophy applies across these surfaces; their wording can serve different readers. Use concrete examples of useful action and checked results. Other phrases may explain a specific feature, but do not replace the primary slogan.

West Coast KBP remains the company attribution. The GitHub organization display name is KBP AI; its technical login remains WestCoastKBP. Product naming does not rename a company, account namespace or repository by itself.
