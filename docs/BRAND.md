# KBP AI — brand and philosophy

Adopted by the Owner on October 10, 2026.

## Name and slogan

The product name is **KBP AI**.

**KBP** stands for **Knowledge Becomes Progress**. This is also the primary slogan; keep this English wording consistent.

**AI** stands for **Artificial Intelligence**.

## Purpose

Our purpose is to help people turn knowledge and AI into useful actions and checked results in real life.

Knowledge becomes progress when it helps someone understand a matter, choose an action, carry it through and check what happened. A completed task or a useful skill is a result. Generating more messages, code or plans alone is not proof of progress.

The person keeps control of decisions, data and permissions. Personal, engineering and business/work areas share this purpose while retaining their own data and authority boundaries.

## Using the identity

Use **KBP AI** as the product name and **Knowledge Becomes Progress** as the primary slogan in the application, website, GitHub profile, README introductions, demonstrations and public materials. Explain AI as Artificial Intelligence when spelling out the name.

The same philosophy applies across these surfaces; their wording can serve different readers. Use concrete examples of useful action and checked results. Other phrases may explain a specific feature, but do not replace the primary slogan.

West Coast KBP remains the company attribution. The GitHub organization display name is KBP AI; its technical login remains WestCoastKBP. Product naming does not rename a company, account namespace or repository by itself.
