# KBP AI — adopted typographic identity

Owner adopted the KBP AI wordmark direction and authorized rollout on October 10, 2026. The primary mark is the full name on one line: dense bold KBP, a deliberate gap, and thin AI at the same cap height. The previous K/point concept is rejected.

## Brand standard

- Name: KBP AI. Written spelling remains KBP AI even when lettering is rendered as outlines.
- Primary slogan and KBP expansion: Knowledge Becomes Progress.
- AI expansion: Artificial Intelligence. Explain it in prose when useful; do not add it to avatars.
- The primary wordmark contains only KBP AI. Use the companion signature with Knowledge Becomes Progress on larger covers, cards and presentation openings.
- All five letters remain present in messenger and social avatars. No replacement by an isolated K, dot, brain or robot.
- The new vector geometry is the production master. It follows the selected raster direction; the raster concept is a reference, not vector artwork.
- Light: graphite #202123 on white. Dark: #f2f3f5 on #191a1c. App placement uses currentColor and existing semantic tokens. Do not change the app font or control palette to match a logo.
- Keep letter proportions, gap and stroke contrast identical across exports. Resize uniformly; do not retype KBP AI with an arbitrary font, stretch, add gradients or animate the letters.
- Avatar canvas is square with a centered wordmark. The full wordmark bounding box lies inside the circular crop. Opening an avatar displays the larger uploaded image; platform behavior is not a brand-controlled animation.
- Avatar width is 82% of canvas. Maskable PWA exports use a separate 76% version to respect the smaller safe circle.
- Minimum recommended profile display: 48px. 24px is a stress test, and 16px browser favicons lose detail. Never claim perfect five-letter readability at every size.
- Keep the accessible name KBP AI and a separate Owner workspace label where needed. A branded personal avatar does not rename Anton or make his account an autonomous assistant.

## Available exports

Use [identity-v1](../assets/identity-v1) for the current wordmark SVGs, square 1024px avatar PNGs, companion signatures and 1200 × 630 social card. Logo lettering is vector paths. The companion slogan uses outlined DejaVu Sans, with its license included.

KBP AI remains a prototype in development. The brand identifies the project; it does not establish a working installer, public product release or vendor partnership. See [current status](STATUS.md).
